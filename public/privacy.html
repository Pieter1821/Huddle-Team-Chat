Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Huddle is a team chat that each organisation runs on its own Cloudflare account. This policy explains what this Huddle stores, why, and the choices you have. The organisation that set up this Huddle (the “workspace owner”) decides how it is used and is responsible for the data in it. Questions or requests go to the workspace owner or an admin of this workspace.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, the name you choose, your role, and when you joined.
  • What you share: messages, threads, reactions, mentions and files you upload, in the conversations you take part in.
  • Settings: your notification and email preferences, time zone, and devices you turned notifications on for.
  • Security records: sign-in events and admin actions, with the IP address and browser they came from, so admins can spot misuse.

How it is used

Only to run this Huddle: to sign you in, deliver and show messages, send the notifications and email summaries you choose, and keep the workspace secure. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for their own purposes. There are no analytics or tracking scripts.

Where it is kept

Everything is stored in the workspace owner’s own Cloudflare account (database and private file storage). Files are only available to people with access to the conversation they were shared in.

Services this Huddle relies on

  • Cloudflare hosts and runs this Huddle and stores its data.
  • Google (Gmail) sends sign-in codes, invitations and email summaries from the workspace’s Gmail account, if one is set up, using an app password. Huddle only sends: it never reads, stores or deletes mail in that mailbox.
  • Your browser’s push service (for example Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft) delivers notifications if you turn them on. They are encrypted so the push service can’t read them.

How long it is kept

As long as the workspace exists, unless deleted sooner. Deleted messages have their text removed straight away. Sign-in codes expire after 5 minutes. Files that were uploaded but never sent are removed within a day.

Your choices

  • Change your name and email preferences, or pause notifications, in Your settings.
  • Edit or delete your own messages at any time.
  • Unsubscribe from email summaries using the link in any summary.
  • Ask the workspace owner to export or delete your data, or to remove your account.

Security

Sign-in uses one-time codes from an authenticator app or email (no passwords to leak), connections are encrypted, and stored secrets such as the Gmail connection are encrypted at rest. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong, the workspace owner will tell affected people as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
