The first person to sign in is the workspace owner. This page shows how to sign in with your setup key and an authenticator app, and how to name your workspace.

## Sign in as the owner

Open your Huddle address. On a fresh install the page is titled **Set up Huddle**.

1. In **Your email**, type the `ADMIN_EMAIL` you deployed with.
2. In **Setup key**, paste the setup key you made when you deployed (the `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET` setting).
3. Select **Continue**.
4. Open your authenticator app and add an account by scanning the QR code. If you can't scan it, open **Can’t scan? Enter this key instead** and type the key into the app. Choose a **time based** code.
5. Type the 6-digit code the app now shows for Huddle, then select **Confirm**.

You're signed in. Nobody else can do this, because it needs both your email address and your setup key.

<Figure name="owner-setup-authenticator-desktop" caption="Setting up an authenticator app: a QR code to scan and a box for the code it shows." />

If you set up [email](/docs/email) before your first sign-in, the page offers an emailed code instead: type your `ADMIN_EMAIL`, select **Email me a sign-in code** and enter the 6-digit code from your inbox. You can add an authenticator app afterwards in **Your settings**.

## Name your workspace

Next, Huddle asks for two things:

- **Workspace name**: your company or team, for example *Northwind Studio*.
- **Your name**: how your team will see you.

Select **Create workspace**. Huddle creates the workspace, makes you its **owner**, and creates **#general**, the channel everyone joins.

You can change the workspace name later in **Admin › Workspace**, and your own name in **Your settings**.

<Clip name="owner-setup" caption="Setting up a new Huddle: setup key, authenticator app, workspace name, then #general." />

## After that

From now on you sign in on the normal sign-in page with your email and the current code from your authenticator app. See [Signing in](/docs/signing-in) for the details, including what to do on a new phone.

<Callout>If you lose the phone with your authenticator app, open `/signin?owner=recover` on your Huddle address. It asks for your email and the setup key again and lets you set up a new phone. The link isn't shown anywhere on the sign-in page on purpose.</Callout>
