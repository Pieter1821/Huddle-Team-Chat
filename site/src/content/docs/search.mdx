Search finds messages, files, channels and people across your workspace. This page explains how to search and how matching works.

## Open search

Press **Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K** on a Mac) anywhere in Huddle, or select **Search** at the top of the sidebar. Start typing: results appear as you go.

<Figure name="search-desktop" caption="Search results grouped into channels, people, messages and files." />

Results are grouped into:

- **Channels** whose name matches.
- **People** whose name matches.
- **Messages**, newest first, with the matching words highlighted.
- **Files** whose name matches.

Select a result to go straight to it. A channel opens the channel, a person opens your direct message with them, and a message opens its conversation, or its thread if it's a reply.

## How matching works

- **Every word must match.** *invoice march* finds messages containing both words.
- **Endings are filled in.** *kitch* finds *kitchen*, and *deploy* finds *deployed*.
- Punctuation and symbols are ignored, and case doesn't matter.
- Up to eight words are used.

<Clip name="search" caption="Pressing Ctrl+K, searching and opening the matching message." />

## What you can find

Search only ever looks at what you can read:

- Conversations you're a member of.
- Public channels, even ones you haven't joined, unless you're a guest.
- Never private channels or DMs you're not in. They aren't even counted.

Deleted messages, and files from deleted messages, never appear. Guests only find people they share a channel with.

## More filters for developers

Behind the scenes, search can also narrow results to one conversation, one person, messages with files, or a range of dates. The app doesn't have buttons for these yet; developers can use them through the [API](/docs/api).
