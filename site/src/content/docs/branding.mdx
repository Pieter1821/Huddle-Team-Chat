Owners and admins can make Huddle look like their own. This page covers the settings in **Admin › Workspace**.

## Name

Type a new **Name** and select **Save**. It appears in the sidebar, on the sign-in page, in the browser tab, in emails and in the authenticator app entry of anyone who sets one up afterwards.

## Logo

Select **Upload a logo** and pick an image. It's shown on the sign-in page and in emails.

- PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 1 MB.
- A wide logo about 64 pixels tall works best.
- SVG isn't accepted, because the logo is shown to people who aren't signed in and SVG files can carry scripts.

Select **Remove** to go back to the Huddle logo.

## Brand colour

The brand colour is used for buttons, links and highlights.

1. Under **Brand colour**, pick a colour or type a colour code such as `#00b18c`.
2. Huddle shows the contrast with the dark button text. It needs at least **4.5:1**, so text on buttons stays readable.
3. Select **Save colour**.

Colours that are too dark are refused with a suggestion to try a lighter shade. Select **Use Huddle’s default** to go back to Huddle's teal.

## Guests

Tick **Members can invite guests** to allow members, not only owners and admins, to invite guests. See [People and roles](/docs/roles-and-permissions).

Every change on this page is recorded in the security log, with what it changed from and to.
