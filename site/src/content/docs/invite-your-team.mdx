Huddle is invite-only. This page shows how to invite people, what they see when they open the link, and how to withdraw an invitation.

## Send an invitation

Owners and admins invite people from **Admin › People**.

1. Select the **Admin** icon at the bottom of the sidebar, then **People**.
2. Under **Invite people**, type the person's **Email**.
3. Choose a **Role**: **Member**, **Guest** or (owners only) **Admin**. See [People and roles](/docs/roles-and-permissions) for what each can do.
4. For a guest, pick the **Channels the guest can see**. Guests need at least one.
5. Select **Send invitation**.

## Share the link

What happens next depends on whether [email](/docs/email) is set up:

- **With email**, Huddle emails the invitation and shows **Invitation emailed to** followed by the address.
- **Without email**, Huddle shows the **Invitation link** with a **Copy** button. Send it to the person yourself, in any chat or email you like.

Each invitation works once and expires after **7 days**. Inviting the same address again replaces the older invitation.

## What the invited person sees

The link opens a page titled **Join** followed by your workspace name, saying who invited them. To accept, they confirm it's them:

- If email is set up, Huddle sends a 6-digit code to the invited address. They can choose **Use an authenticator app instead**.
- If email isn't set up, they set up an authenticator app straight away: scan the QR code and type the code it shows.

Then they choose the name people will see. Members and admins start in **#general**; guests start in the channels you picked.

If a link has expired, was used already or was withdrawn, the page says **This invitation can’t be used** and asks them to get a new one.

## Pending invitations

Invitations that haven't been accepted yet appear under **Pending invitations**, with the role and expiry date. Select **Withdraw** to cancel one: the link stops working at once.
