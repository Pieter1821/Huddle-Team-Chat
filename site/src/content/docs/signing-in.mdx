Huddle has no passwords. You sign in with a 6-digit code, either from an authenticator app on your phone or sent to your email. This page explains both, and what to do when you change or lose your phone.

## With an authenticator app

This always works, even if your Huddle doesn't send email.

1. Open your Huddle address. If the page offers an emailed code, select **Use an authenticator app instead**.
2. Type your **Work email**.
3. Type the current 6-digit **Code from your authenticator app**.
4. Select **Sign in**.

<Figure name="signin-authenticator-desktop" caption="Signing in with your email and the code from your authenticator app." />

An authenticator app is a free phone app that shows a new 6-digit code every 30 seconds. Any of the common ones works: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy, 1Password and others. Each code works only once, and it doesn't matter if your phone's clock is a few seconds off.

## With an emailed code

If email is set up, the sign-in page starts with this.

1. Type your **Work email** and select **Email me a sign-in code**.
2. Type the 6-digit **Sign-in code** from the email and select **Sign in**.

The code works once, for 5 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, wait 30 seconds and select **Send a new code**, and check your spam folder.

## With Google or GitHub

If your Huddle has [Sign in with Google](/docs/google-sign-in) or [Sign in with GitHub](/docs/github-sign-in) turned on, the sign-in page also shows **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**. Select one and choose your account. You still need to have been invited: if that account's email wasn't, Huddle tells you to ask an admin.

## How long you stay signed in

You stay signed in on each device for 30 days, renewed while you keep using Huddle. Select **Sign out** (at the bottom of the sidebar) to sign out of that device.

## Set up or change your authenticator app

In **Your settings › Authenticator app**:

- **Set up** adds an authenticator app if you joined with an emailed code.
- **Move to a new phone** sets up the app on your new phone. Your old phone keeps working until you confirm the new one.
- **Turn off** removes it. You can then only sign in with emailed codes, so only do this if your Huddle sends email.

## If you lose your phone

Ask an owner or admin for a **reset link**. They create it in **Admin › People** with **Reset app**. The link works once, for 7 days, and lets you set up the app on your new phone. Your old app keeps working until you use it.

If you're the owner, open `/signin?owner=recover` on your Huddle address and sign in again with your email and the setup key.

## Keep the manual key safe

When you set up the app, **Can’t scan? Enter this key instead** shows the secret behind your codes. Anyone with that key can make your codes. Type it into your authenticator app and nowhere else: don't screenshot it, email it or keep it in a note.

## Too many wrong codes

After 8 wrong authenticator codes in a row, signing in with the app is locked for 15 minutes. Emailed codes allow 3 tries each, and you can ask for up to 5 codes an hour. Failed attempts are recorded in the security log, so admins can spot someone guessing.

## Deactivated accounts

If an admin deactivates your account, you're signed out everywhere and can't sign in until they reactivate you.
