Huddle works in any web browser, and you can also install it like an app. It then opens in its own window, like your other apps, and can show notifications. There's nothing to download from an app store.

## On a computer

In Chrome or Edge:

1. Open your Huddle address and sign in.
2. Open **Your settings** (your name at the bottom of the sidebar).
3. Under **This device**, select **Install Huddle**.

The button only appears when the browser offers installing. You can also use the install icon in the browser's address bar.

## On Android

1. Open your Huddle address in Chrome and sign in.
2. Open the browser menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. You can also use **Install Huddle** in **Your settings**.

## On iPhone and iPad

1. Open your Huddle address in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Huddle from the new icon on your Home Screen.

<Callout>On iPhone and iPad, notifications only work in the installed app. Open Huddle from the Home Screen icon before you turn them on.</Callout>

## Turn on notifications

Notifications are turned on for each device separately.

1. Open **Your settings**.
2. Under **This device**, switch on **Notifications on this device** and allow notifications when the browser asks.
3. Select **Send a test notification** to check it works.

You're notified when someone mentions you, sends you a direct message or replies in a thread you're in. See [Notifications](/docs/notifications) to choose what reaches you.

If the browser says notifications are blocked, allow them in the site settings (the icon next to the address), then reload.

## Offline

When there's no connection, Huddle shows a short **You’re offline** page instead of the browser's error. Select **Try again** once you're back online. Huddle doesn't keep a copy of your messages on the device.
