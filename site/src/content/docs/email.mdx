Email is optional. Without it, invitations are links you share yourself and everyone signs in with an authenticator app. With it, Huddle also emails invitations, sign-in codes and summaries of missed messages. This page shows how to set it up with a Gmail account in about five minutes.

## How it works

Huddle sends email from a normal Gmail account. To let it, you create an **app password**: a special password Google gives you for one app, separate from your real password. You don't need any other email service or technical setup. A free Gmail account can send about 500 emails a day, which is plenty for a small team.

Huddle only sends from the account. It never reads, stores or deletes mail in it.

## Set it up

1. Sign in to the Gmail account that should send Huddle's email. A dedicated one, such as `northwind.huddle@gmail.com`, is best.
2. Go to **myaccount.google.com › Security** and turn on **2-Step Verification** if it isn't on yet. Google only offers app passwords with it on.
3. Open **myaccount.google.com/apppasswords**, create one named **Huddle**, and copy the 16-letter password.
4. In Cloudflare, open **Workers & Pages › your Worker › Settings › Variables and Secrets** and add two **Secrets**:
   - `GMAIL_ADDRESS`: the Gmail address
   - `GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD`: the 16-letter password
5. Deploy, wait a minute and reload Huddle.

Optionally, add `EMAIL_FROM` to choose the name people see on Huddle's emails, for example `Northwind Huddle <x@example.com>`. Only the name is used.

## Check it works

Open **Admin › Email**. It now says **Sending from** your Gmail address. Select **Send me a test email**; it goes to your own address.

## What Huddle sends

| Email | When |
| --- | --- |
| Invitation | When an owner or admin invites someone |
| Sign-in code | When someone asks for a code on the sign-in page |
| Email summary | When someone has unread mentions, direct messages or thread replies (see [Notifications](/docs/notifications)) |
| Test email | When you select **Send me a test email** |

Sign-in codes only go to people who could actually sign in: existing members, or people with a pending invitation. Anyone else gets the same "sent" message on screen, but no email, so the sign-in page can't be used to find out who is a member or to use up your daily sending.

## The daily budget

Huddle counts every email it sends. Once 80 have gone out in a day, it stops sending summaries until the next day, so sign-in codes and invitations always have room. **Admin › Usage** shows today's count under **Emails sent today**.

## Change or remove the account

Update `GMAIL_ADDRESS` and `GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD` in Cloudflare and deploy. To turn email off, delete both. To stop Huddle's access from Google's side, delete the app password at **myaccount.google.com/apppasswords**.

## If email doesn't arrive

- Check the spam folder, and mark the message as not spam. Sending from a dedicated Gmail that people add to their contacts helps.
- Make sure 2-Step Verification is still on for the Gmail account. Turning it off deletes its app passwords.
- Use **Send me a test email**: if it fails, the message says why.
