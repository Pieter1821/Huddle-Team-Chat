Threads let you answer one message without filling the channel. This page shows how to start, follow and share a thread.

## Reply in a thread

1. Hover over a message (or move to it with the keyboard) and select **Reply in thread**.
2. The thread opens beside the conversation (on a phone, it opens full screen).
3. Type your reply in the **Reply…** box and press **Enter**.

The message in the channel now shows how many replies it has, for example **3 replies**. Select it to open the thread again.

<Figure name="thread-desktop" caption="A thread open beside the channel." />

## Also send to the channel

Under the reply box, tick **Also send to** followed by the channel's name to post your reply in the channel as well. Use it for conclusions everyone should see. In the channel, the message is marked **Also replied in a thread**.

<Clip name="reply-in-thread" caption="Opening a thread, mentioning someone and sending a reply." />

## Who gets notified

When someone replies in a thread, Huddle notifies the person who wrote the first message and everyone who has replied so far, as long as they're still in the conversation. Mentions in a reply notify the people mentioned, as usual.

Thread replies don't add to the channel's unread count, unless they were also sent to the channel.

## Close a thread

Select **Close thread**, or on a phone **Back to the conversation**. Anything you had typed stays as a draft for that thread while Huddle is open.
