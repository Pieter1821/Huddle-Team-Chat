Something not working? Find the message you're seeing, or the problem you have, below. Each one says what it means and what to do.

## Setting up

### The sign-in page says "Almost there"

One of Huddle's settings is missing or not quite right, and the message tells you which one.

1. In Cloudflare, open **Workers & Pages**, select your Huddle, then **Settings › Variables and Secrets**.
2. Fix the setting it named. The two most common mistakes are a `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET` (setup key) shorter than 32 characters, and a typo in `ADMIN_EMAIL`.
3. Wait a minute and reload.

The [settings reference](/docs/settings-reference) shows what each setting should look like.

### "Huddle couldn’t set up its database"

Huddle couldn't reach its database. In Cloudflare, open your Huddle, then **Settings › Bindings**, and check there's a D1 database connected with the name `DB`. Reload once it's there: Huddle tries again by itself.

### The setup stops at file storage

Cloudflare needs a payment card before it switches on file storage (called R2). Open **R2 Object Storage** in the Cloudflare dashboard, add a card and switch R2 on, then run the setup again. You won't be charged for normal use: storage is free up to 10 GB.

### "That setup key and email don’t match this Huddle"

For the owner's first sign-in, both boxes must match what was entered when Huddle was set up:

- **Your email** must be the `ADMIN_EMAIL` setting. Capital letters don't matter.
- **Setup key** must be the whole `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`. Copy and paste it rather than typing it.

## Signing in

### "That code didn’t match" or "That email and code don’t match"

- Type the newest code your authenticator app shows for Huddle, before it changes.
- Check your phone's clock is set automatically (in your phone's date and time settings).
- If your app has more than one Huddle in it, make sure you're using the right one.

After 8 wrong codes in a row, signing in with the app is paused for 15 minutes.

### "This setup expired. Start again."

Setting up an authenticator app has to be finished within 15 minutes. Reload the page and scan the new QR code.

### Someone lost their phone

An owner or admin opens **Admin › People**, selects **Reset app** next to the person's name, and sends them the link. If the owner lost their phone, they go to `/signin?owner=recover` on the Huddle address and use the setup key. See [Signing in](/docs/signing-in#if-you-lose-your-phone).

### "This email has not been invited to this workspace"

Huddle is invite-only. Ask an owner or admin to [invite](/docs/invite-your-team) that exact email address.

### "This account has been deactivated" or "Your guest access has ended"

An admin switched the account off, or a guest's access date has passed. An owner or admin can switch it back on with **Reactivate** in **Admin › People**.

### "Cross-site requests are not allowed"

You're probably using an old address for Huddle. This happens after moving to [your own domain](/docs/custom-domain): the old `workers.dev` address no longer lets anyone sign in or post. Open Huddle at its new address.

## Email

### Emails don't arrive, or land in spam

- Look in the spam or junk folder, and mark Huddle's email as "not spam".
- In **Admin › Email**, select **Send me a test email**. If it fails, the message says why.
- Check that 2-Step Verification is still on for the Gmail account. Switching it off deletes the app password Huddle uses.
- If **Admin › Usage** shows today's emails are used up, summaries wait until tomorrow. Sign-in codes and invitations still go out.

### "Email is not set up for this workspace yet"

Huddle doesn't have a Gmail account to send from. Follow the steps in [Email](/docs/email), or sign in with an authenticator app instead.

## Notifications

### Notifications don't appear

1. In **Your settings › This device**, check **Notifications on this device** is on, then select **Send a test notification**.
2. On iPhone and iPad, open Huddle from its icon on your Home Screen. Notifications don't work in Safari itself.
3. If your browser blocked notifications, allow them in the site settings (the icon to the left of the web address), then reload.
4. Check you haven't paused notifications in **Your settings**, and that the conversation isn't set to **Muted**.
5. Check your phone or computer allows notifications from your browser or the Huddle app.

If the test says the browser turned notifications off, switch them on again.

## Everyday use

### "You’re sending messages very quickly"

Each person can send about 60 messages a minute. Wait a moment and send again.

### A channel has disappeared

It may have been archived, or someone may have removed you. Private channels only show to the people in them, so ask someone inside to add you back.

## Still stuck?

Cloudflare keeps a record of errors. Open **Workers & Pages**, select your Huddle, then **Logs**. If a setting is the problem, the log names it (but never shows its value). You can also [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Pieter1821/Huddle-Team-Chat/issues) to ask for help. Don't paste your setup key or passwords there.
