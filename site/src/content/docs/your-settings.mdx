Everything about your own account is in one place. Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open **Your settings**.

<Figure name="settings-desktop" caption="Your settings on a desktop browser." />

## Profile

- **Name** is how you appear next to your messages and in mentions. Change it and select **Save**. Your old mentions update to the new name automatically.
- **Email** is the address you sign in with. You can't change it yourself; if it changes, an admin can invite your new address.

## Authenticator app

Set up an authenticator app, move it to a new phone, or turn it off. See [Signing in](/docs/signing-in).

## This device

Turn notifications on or off for the phone or computer you're using, send a test notification, and install Huddle as an app. See [Install the app](/docs/install-the-app).

## Email notifications

Choose how often Huddle emails you a summary of what you missed, how long it waits first, your time zone, and pause everything for a while. See [Notifications](/docs/notifications).

## Privacy policy and terms

Links to this Huddle's **privacy policy** and **terms of use** are at the bottom of **Your settings** and on the sign-in page. They describe what is stored and that the organisation running the workspace is responsible for it.
