Sign in with Google is optional. It adds a **Continue with Google** button under the emailed code, so people who already use a Google account don't need a code or an authenticator app. Nobody is added automatically: only people who have been [invited](/docs/invite-your-team) can get in, and a Google account that wasn't invited sees a message telling them to ask an admin.

You need your Huddle's web address to be final first. If you're going to use [your own web address](/docs/custom-domain), set that up before you start, because Google checks the address exactly.

## Create the Google sign-in

This takes about ten minutes and costs nothing. Use a Google account you're happy to own this, ideally one that isn't your personal one.

1. Go to **console.cloud.google.com** and create a project named `Huddle`.
2. Open **APIs & Services › OAuth consent screen** (it may be called **Google Auth Platform**) and choose **External**.
3. Fill in the app name (`Huddle`), a support email and a contact email. A free Google Group works well as the support email, so your own address isn't shown to people.
4. Under **Branding**, add your Huddle's home page, privacy page and terms page. Huddle serves these at `/privacy` and `/terms` on its own address. Add your domain under **Authorized domains**.
5. Under **Audience**, select **Publish app**. If you leave it in testing, only people you list as test users can sign in. Huddle asks Google only for the basics (`openid`, `email` and `profile`), which need no review by Google.
6. Open **Credentials** (or **Clients**) and create an **OAuth client ID** of type **Web application**.
7. Under **Authorized JavaScript origins**, add your Huddle's address, for example `https://chat.example.com`.
8. Under **Authorized redirect URIs**, add the same address followed by `/api/auth/callback/google`, for example `https://chat.example.com/api/auth/callback/google`. It must match exactly, with no slash at the end.
9. Create it, then copy the **Client ID** and **Client secret**.

## Give them to Huddle

In Cloudflare, open your Huddle's **Settings** and add two secrets, using exactly these names:

| Name | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` | The Client ID from Google |
| `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` | The Client secret from Google |

Also make sure `APP_URL` is your Huddle's address (see [settings reference](/docs/settings-reference)). Save, then reload the sign-in page. The **Continue with Google** button appears once both secrets are set.

## What people see

Someone you've invited selects **Continue with Google**, chooses their account and lands in Huddle. If the Google account's email isn't one that was invited, they come back to the sign-in page with a message. Deactivated people can't sign in this way either.

## Turn it off

Delete the two secrets and reload. The button disappears and nothing else changes. To stop it from Google's side too, delete the OAuth client in the Google Cloud project.

## If the button doesn't appear or fails

- **Any failure:** a failed Google sign-in is recorded in **Admin › Security log** as "Sign-in failed", with the provider and an error code. Check there first, and send the code when asking for help.
- **No button:** one of the two secrets is missing or misspelled. The names must be exactly `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` and `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Google says "redirect_uri_mismatch":** the redirect address in Google doesn't match your Huddle's address character for character. Check `https`, the domain and `/api/auth/callback/google`.
- **Google says the app isn't verified, or blocks the account:** the app is still in testing. Publish it under **Audience**.
- **"Hasn't been invited":** invite that exact email address first, then try again.
